If you want Dubai chocolate in Clear Lake, you do not have to drive into the middle of Houston for it. Dubai & Dips is a dessert shop in Clear Lake built around exactly those flavors: chocolate, pistachio and kunafa, served cold in a cup or warm and crisp on a plate. Here is what to order, when to come, and how to make the trip easy.

## What "Dubai chocolate" means on our menu

Dubai chocolate started as a filled chocolate bar: a chocolate shell around pistachio cream and toasted, shredded kunafa pastry. If you are new to it, our [explainer on Dubai chocolate](/blog/dubai-chocolate-houston-explained) covers where it came from and what it tastes like.

At Dubai & Dips we take those flavors and put them on our board as flights. Every category on [our menu](/menu) is a route, and the Dubai route is where it starts.

## Dubai chocolate in Clear Lake: what to order

### The Dubai Chocolate Frappe

The first drink on the [frappes route](/menu#frappes) is the **Dubai Chocolate Frappe**. It is blended and served cold, then finished with whipped cream and a drizzle of both pistachio and dark chocolate. It is the drink on our own feature board, the one we point first-time visitors to.

### Kunafa, for the crunch

Dubai chocolate is as much about texture as flavor. If you want the crisp half, order the **kunafa** from our [D&D Desserts route](/menu#desserts): a round of golden pastry threads topped with crushed pistachio. It pairs naturally with the frappe: cold and smooth next to warm and crisp.

### More pistachio on the board

If pistachio is what drew you in, there is plenty more of it:

- **Pistachio Frappe** on the [frappes route](/menu#frappes), topped with whipped cream and green pistachio sauce.
- **Pistachio Matcha** on the [matcha route](/menu#matchas).
- **Pistachio Latte** on the [lattes route](/menu#lattes).

Not sure which to choose? Our [frappe guide](/blog/pistachio-frappe-houston-which-to-order) compares the Pistachio, Biscoff and Dubai Chocolate frappes side by side.

## When to come

Our Clear Lake shop keeps long hours:

- **Monday to Thursday:** 9 AM to 10 PM
- **Friday and Saturday:** 9 AM to midnight
- **Sunday:** 10 AM to 10 PM

That makes a Dubai chocolate run easy to fit around work, school or a night out. On Friday and Saturday nights we are open until midnight, which is handy after dinner or a movie; our [late-night dessert guide](/blog/late-night-dessert-houston) has more on that.

## Make it an evening

Dubai & Dips is a room built to linger in, not just a counter. A deep green wall carries the gold DUBAI & DIPS sign over the pastry counter, with marble tables and white chairs in front of it. It works for a coffee catch-up in the morning, a study break in the afternoon, or dessert after dinner. Order your Dubai Chocolate Frappe, take a table, and share a kunafa while you are there.

## A first-visit plan

If this is your first time, here is a simple way to do it:

1. **Order the Dubai Chocolate Frappe.** It is the flavor everyone comes in for.
2. **Share a kunafa.** It brings the crunch that makes Dubai chocolate what it is.
3. **Ask at the counter.** If you are torn between drinks, we are happy to help you pick. Can't decide? Come in for a sample.
