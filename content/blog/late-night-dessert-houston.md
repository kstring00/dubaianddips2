Finding late night dessert in Houston usually means settling for whatever is still open. The movie ended, dinner ran long, or the craving simply showed up at 10:30 PM. At Dubai & Dips in Clear Lake, the weekend board stays lit until midnight, so a proper dessert does not have to wait until tomorrow.

## Our hours, and why the weekend runs late

Here is when the gate is open at our [Clear Lake shop](/visit/clear-lake):

| Day | Hours |
| --- | --- |
| Monday to Thursday | 9 AM to 10 PM |
| Friday and Saturday | 9 AM to midnight |
| Sunday | 10 AM to 10 PM |

Fridays and Saturdays are the late flights: the doors stay open until midnight. The rest of the week we close at 10 PM, still later than a lot of dessert spots.

The [visit page](/visit/clear-lake) shows whether we are open right now and how long until closing, worked out in Houston time, so you can check before you head out.

## Late night dessert in Houston: what to order

Late-night dessert has its own rules. You want something that feels like a treat, but you might not want a triple espresso at 11 PM. Here is how we would steer you through [the menu](/menu).

### Cold and indulgent: the frappes

The [frappes route](/menu#frappes) is where most late visits start. They are blended, cold and rich:

- **Dubai Chocolate Frappe**, with pistachio and dark chocolate drizzle and whipped cream.
- **Pistachio Frappe**, finished with green pistachio sauce.
- **Biscoff Frappe**, with cookie butter swirled through and a whole Biscoff cookie on top.
- **Salted Caramel Frappe.**

Our [frappe guide](/blog/pistachio-frappe-houston-which-to-order) compares the Pistachio, Biscoff and Dubai Chocolate frappes if you want to pick before you arrive.

### Fruit-forward: smoothies

If you want something lighter, the [smoothies route](/menu#smoothies) has Strawberry, Strawberry Banana, Mango and Banana Date smoothies.

### Warm and crisp: crepes, waffles and kunafa

Some nights call for something warm. The [crepes](/menu#crepes) and [waffles](/menu#waffles) routes are on the boards in the shop, and the [D&D Desserts route](/menu#desserts) has **kunafa**: crisp golden pastry topped with crushed pistachio. It is a good one to share across the table.

### Matcha, if you still want a lift

If you are heading to something rather than home, the [matcha route](/menu#matchas) is long: Strawberry, Pistachio, Biscoff, Mango, Banana, Blueberry Cream and Banana Date matchas, plus a 12 oz Matcha Cortado.

### Coffee, if the night is young

The [classic coffees route](/menu#classic-coffees) covers the espresso side of the menu: Espresso, Cappuccino, Americano, Cortado and Affogato, plus Leche y Leche, a Spanish-style cortado. An Affogato makes a neat bridge between coffee and dessert, and the [lattes route](/menu#lattes) has sweeter options such as the Spanish Latte and the Tiramisu Latte.

## A late-night order for two

Sharing is the best way to do late-night dessert. A simple order for two: one Dubai Chocolate Frappe, one Biscoff Frappe and a kunafa in the middle of the table. Swap drinks halfway, and you have tried three different sides of the menu in one visit.

## Plan it: late-night timing tips

- **Aim for before 11:30 PM on weekends.** We are open until midnight, but arriving with a little room means you are never rushed.
- **Order ahead if you are on the way.** Order ahead for pickup and it will be ready when you walk in.
- **Check the live status.** Holidays can change the hours; the [visit page](/visit/clear-lake) always shows the hours for today.

## Why the room is worth staying in

Late-night dessert is better when there is somewhere good to sit. The shop is a room built to linger in: a deep green wall with the gold DUBAI & DIPS sign over the counter, marble tables and white chairs. It is the kind of place that works for a late date, a catch-up after work, or the last stop of a night out.

Parking is easy: there is a free lot right out front, with more spaces around the side of the building.
