Ordering dessert for an office sounds simple until you are the one doing it. How many drinks? What if half the team does not drink coffee? Will it survive the drive? This guide walks through dessert catering in Houston step by step, using what we have learned from how group orders work at our dessert shop in Clear Lake.

## Step 1: Decide what kind of moment it is

The right order depends on what you are celebrating.

- **A thank-you or a win.** Something indulgent that feels like a treat: frappes and a few desserts to share.
- **A morning meeting.** Coffee and matcha drinks do the heavy lifting; add something sweet on the side.
- **A birthday or farewell.** A mix, so everyone finds something they like.
- **An all-day event or training.** Plan for two rounds, one in the morning and one in the afternoon.

## Step 2: Count your people, then count the drinkers

Start with the headcount, then think about who drinks what. In most offices there are coffee people, tea people and people who want something sweet and cold. A mixed order covers all three.

A simple way to split it:

1. **Coffee drinkers:** espresso drinks from the [classic coffees route](/menu#classic-coffees): Espresso, Cappuccino, Americano, Cortado, Affogato and Leche y Leche.
2. **Matcha and lighter options:** the [matcha route](/menu#matchas) and the [smoothies route](/menu#smoothies).
3. **The treat crowd:** the [frappes route](/menu#frappes), including the Dubai Chocolate, Pistachio and Biscoff frappes.

## Step 5: Plan the drop

A few practical tips for the day itself:

- **Time it to the moment.** Cold drinks are best right away, so time pickup or delivery for when people are ready.
- **Label it.** If people chose their own drinks, keep your list handy at pickup so everyone finds theirs.
- **Think about allergies.** Many of our most popular drinks feature pistachio. Ask us about ingredients when you order. <!-- OWNER TO CONFIRM: allergen information to share with office customers. -->
- **Add something to share.** A few desserts from the [D&D Desserts route](/menu#desserts), such as kunafa, turn a drinks order into a proper treat.
