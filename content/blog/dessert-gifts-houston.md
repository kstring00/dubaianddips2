Flowers wilt and gift cards get forgotten, but a surprise dessert gets talked about. If you are looking for dessert gifts in Houston for a birthday, a thank-you or a whole team, here are ideas built around what is on the board at Dubai & Dips in Clear Lake, plus the practical bits: timing, pickup and delivery.

## Why dessert makes a good gift

A dessert gift does three things at once. It is personal, because you picked something you know they will love. It is an experience, because it gets opened and shared right away. And it is easy to get right, because almost everyone has a favorite treat.

The trick is matching the dessert to the person.

## Dessert gifts in Houston, matched to the person

### For the one who follows every food trend: Dubai chocolate flavors

If they have been sending you videos of green-filled chocolate bars, they want Dubai chocolate. Our **Dubai Chocolate Frappe** from the [frappes route](/menu#frappes) brings chocolate and pistachio together, finished with whipped cream and both drizzles. Add a **kunafa** from the [D&D Desserts route](/menu#desserts) for the crunch. Our [Dubai chocolate explainer](/blog/dubai-chocolate-houston-explained) is a nice link to send along with it.

### For the pistachio lover

Pistachio runs all through our menu. Pick from the **Pistachio Frappe**, the **Pistachio Matcha** on the [matcha route](/menu#matchas) or the **Pistachio Latte** on the [lattes route](/menu#lattes).

### For the sweet tooth

The **Biscoff Frappe** has cookie butter swirled through and a whole Biscoff cookie on top. It is the most dessert-like drink on the board and an easy win for anyone who loves caramel.

### For the coffee person

A thank-you for the colleague who covered for you? The [classic coffees route](/menu#classic-coffees) has Espresso, Cappuccino, Americano, Cortado and Affogato, plus Leche y Leche, a Spanish-style cortado. The **Spanish Latte** and **Tiramisu Latte** on the lattes route are more of a treat.

### For the matcha fan

The [matcha route](/menu#matchas) is one of our longest: Strawberry, Pistachio, Biscoff, Mango, Banana, Blueberry Cream and Banana Date matchas, and a 12 oz Matcha Cortado.

### For the whole team

If the thank-you is for a group, make it a group order. Our [catering page](/catering) explains how it works: one link, everyone picks, one payment, and it all arrives together. Group orders start at $50. Our [office dessert guide](/blog/dessert-catering-houston-office) has tips for planning it.

Birthdays deserve something a little theatrical:

## Thank-you gift ideas

A thank-you works best when it arrives unexpectedly:

- **Surprise delivery.** We deliver up to 5 miles from the shop with a $15 minimum, so you can send a thank-you straight to someone's door or desk.
- **Pickup on the way.** Order ahead for pickup, collect it at the counter, and drop it off yourself.
- **A round for the team.** For the people who helped you ship a project, a group order lets everyone pick their own favorite.

## Small gifts that still feel special

Not every gift has to be big. A single drink, picked for the person, says you were paying attention. A Strawberry Matcha for the friend who always orders matcha, a Pistachio Latte for the colleague who loves pistachio, or an Affogato for the person who cannot choose between coffee and dessert. Pair it with a short note and it lands better than most things you could buy.
