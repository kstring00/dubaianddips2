The craft

We make it here.

The gelato is made in this building. Not trucked in frozen from somewhere else. That is the whole reason the texture is what it is when you get it.

Fresh has a short life, so we make what the day needs and we make it again tomorrow. What is in the case in the morning is what went in that morning.

The ingredients are the part people taste without knowing why. Real pistachio, real chocolate, real matcha. You can taste the spot where a shortcut would have been.

The pastry is the same story. Kataifi pulled apart by hand, layered, baked until the edges go, then the pistachio goes on last so it stays green.
