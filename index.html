The craft

One drink, taken apart.

Keep scrolling. Every layer of the matcha comes off, one at a time, so you can see exactly what goes in the cup.

  1. Whipped cream

    Whipped here, plain. Soft, not stiff. It is the first thing you taste, so it has to be fresh.

  2. Caramel

    A thin ribbon across the top, so it is in the first sip without taking over the cup.

  3. Matcha

    Whisked to order, on ice. Grassy, a little bitter, the way it should be. If it only tastes sweet, it was not good matcha.

  4. Milk

    Whole milk, blended cold. It rounds the matcha off and carries it through the whole cup.

  5. That is the whole drink.

    Four things, nothing hidden. Come point at the case and we will build one in front of you.
